Frequently Asked Questions

Does the bank account have to be in the student's name?

Yes. Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) guidelines mandate an active bank savings account in the student's own name, seeded with their Aadhaar number.

Is this scholarship renewable for next academic year?

Yes! Recipients who maintain the required academic score (usually 50% to 60% aggregate) and minimum 75% attendance are automatically eligible for annual renewal.
